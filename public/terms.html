What Investigator™ is

Investigator™ is a free, AI-assisted due diligence tool designed to help members of the public ask better questions, access publicly available information, and think more clearly before making an investment decision.

It works by guiding you through seven structured stages covering the investment itself, client classification, consent and pressure, offshore and jurisdictional risk, public data checks, structural asymmetry, and potential consequences. At the end, it produces a plain-language summary based on your responses.

Investigator™ exists because access to clear, independent thinking about financial decisions should not depend on how much money you already have. It is a tool to restore human agency — not to replace human judgement.

It does not assess the suitability of any investment for your personal circumstances, and nothing produced by this tool should be treated as a personal recommendation to invest or not to invest.

Not legal advice. Nothing produced by Investigator™ constitutes legal advice. If you need legal guidance — for example, if you believe you have been defrauded — consult a solicitor.

Not an FCA-regulated service. Investigator™ is not authorised or regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority. It does not provide regulated financial services and is not required to be FCA-authorised. It provides structured information support and AI-assisted analysis — not regulated advice or intermediation.

Not a guarantee. A positive or reassuring result from Investigator™ does not confirm that an investment is safe, legitimate, or suitable for you. The tool checks what is publicly available and asks questions about what you have been told. It cannot verify claims that are not yet on record, and sophisticated fraud can pass basic checks in its early stages.

AI-assisted tool — important disclosure

Investigator™ uses artificial intelligence to analyse documents you upload and to support the generation of your summary. AI can make errors. It may misread documents, miss information, or produce outputs that are incomplete or inaccurate.

All AI-generated content in Investigator™ is intended to support your own thinking, not to substitute for it. You should verify important information independently and not rely solely on outputs produced by this tool.

Document analysis is performed by the Anthropic Claude API. Documents you upload are sent to Anthropic's servers for processing. They are not stored by Investigator™ or the Academy of Life Planning. Please refer to Anthropic's privacy policy at anthropic.com/privacy for information on how Anthropic handles data submitted to its API.
